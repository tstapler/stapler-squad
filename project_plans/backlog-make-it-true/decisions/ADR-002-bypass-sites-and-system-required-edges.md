# ADR-002: Transition-site inventory and system-required edges (Decision 1 "scope out, but measure" is SUPERSEDED by ADR-007)

**Status**: Partially superseded by [ADR-007](ADR-007-engine-gated-transition-choke-point.md) on 2026-10-17 (owner decision: keep Success Metric 1 strong, route ALL status-write paths through the engine). Decision 1 and the first rejected alternative no longer apply. Decisions 2-5 stay, as amended by ADR-007 (the inventory is now a guard that fails on any raw call; system-required edges cover every `agent` and `system` registry edge; `transitionOrNotify` is injected into `session` through `TransitionDeniedReporter`).
**Date**: 2026-10-17
**Project**: backlog-make-it-true, workstream A

## Context

Enforcement is not centralized (VERIFIED, `research/features.md` Q1.B, `research/architecture.md` Finding 4). Only about six sites consult the engine. Most status writers call `storage.TransitionBacklogItemStatus`, a pure SQL compare-and-swap with no graph or gate check (`session/ent_repository_backlog.go:1721-1760`): `autonomous_orchestration_service.go:586`, MCP `request_review`/`report_blocked`/`resume_work` (`server/mcp/tools_backlog.go:1507,1515,1682,1821`), lifecycle auto-transitions (`session/backlog_lifecycle*.go`), the Jules poller, archive paths.

Recount (repair, `git grep -n "\.TransitionBacklogItemStatus(" -- '*.go' ':!*_test.go' ':!tools/lint'`): 38 call sites in 14 files. Of these: 1 primitive wrapper (`session/storage.go:1104`), 1 MCP delegate to the handler (`tools_backlog_dependencies.go:163`), 5 engine-routed (`backlog_service_lifecycle.go:959`, `backlog_service_triage.go:692` inside `transitionWithGuard`, `backlog_service_lifecycle.go:1332` `OverrideVerdict`, `backlog_service_sync.go:158`, `session/backlog_sync.go:450`), and 31 direct-storage bypass sites in 10 files. The earlier draft listed about 11.

Separately, the earlier draft claimed several callers log-and-continue on a denied transition at `backlog_service_triage.go:801-810`, `:2313-2321` and `session/backlog_lifecycle.go:971-984,1971-1975`. Opened against the current tree, none of those lines is a transition call. The storage-direct sites cannot be denied by the graph at all (they are a SQL compare-and-swap). The only automated caller that swallows an engine denial is `claimAndSpawnCandidate` (`backlog_service_triage.go:837`, `default:` arm `:872-873`, reached through `transitionWithGuard` `:680`); `queueBacklogItem` (`:699`) and `JulesDispatchService` (`jules_dispatch_service.go:361`) return the error to a user-initiated caller, and `SyncLoop` (`backlog_sync.go:449`) skips and retries.

Also, three engine holders exist (BacklogService, lifecycle listener, SyncLoop).

## Decision

1. **[SUPERSEDED by ADR-007] Scope out, but measure.** Operator graph edits govern service-level transitions: the UI RPC, MCP `transition_backlog_item_status` (which delegates to the service, `tools_backlog_dependencies.go:163`), `transitionWithGuard` in triage, `OverrideVerdict`, `AttachSessionToItem`, `SyncLoop`. Direct-storage sites remain on the fixed built-in graph. The exemption is documented on the Stages page ("automated lifecycle edges are fixed") and counted, not assumed: `workflow_engine_bypass_total{caller}`.
2. **Inventory test, not lint.** A Go test (same registry style as ADR-006) walks the WHOLE module with `go/parser` (not just `server/services`: sites live in `session/`, `server/mcp/` and `server/services/`) and lists every non-test call site of `TransitionBacklogItemStatus` by file and function, each classified `engine-routed`, `instrumented(caller)` or `exempt: <reason>` (rollback writes, the debug endpoint, the wrapper); a new site not in the inventory fails the test and forces an explicit engine-or-exempt decision. The inventory is generated first (Task 1.2.3b) and instrumentation follows it. Rejected: a `tools/lint` analyzer (lexical and name-matched; cross-package semantics are poorly suited, `research/build-vs-buy.md` E2).
3. **System-required edges are a code-defined set, not a schema flag.** `session.SystemRequiredEdges()` is derived from BOTH groups. Group 1, engine-checked automation (the union is what actually protects these): `ready->queued` (`queueBacklogItem`), `queued->in_progress` and `ready->in_progress` (`claimAndSpawnCandidate` / `DequeueNextQueuedItems` candidates), `idea->archived`, `refining->archived`, `ready->archived`, `queued->archived` (`SyncLoop`, `determineBackwardSyncTarget` at `session/backlog_sync.go:543`), `->in_progress` for `AttachSessionToItem` (`backlog_service_sync.go:157`). Group 2, storage-direct writers (kept for UI honesty, the union protects nothing for them): `in_progress->review`, `review->done`, `review->pr_pending`, `pr_pending->done`, `review->in_progress`, `pr_pending->in_progress`, `in_progress->ready`, `idea->ready` for triage completion. Exact list fixed by Task 1.2.1a against the call sites. Every edge in Group 1 and Group 2 is system-required and cannot be disabled. **Amended by ADR-007 D4 (Repair round 4)**: system-required means declared by a non-`forwarding` `agent` or `system` registry entry; the generic MCP `transition_backlog_item_status` delegate is `forwarding` and contributes nothing (which is why `done->refining` stays disableable); a third group, the archive edges `X->archived` (every status, including custom slugs) and `archived->archived`, is added to `SystemRequiredEdges()` instead of editing `validTransitions` or the seed. The Story 1.2.4 allow/deny proof deliberately disables a NON-system edge (default `done->refining`) to show the engine denies it, and never disables a system-required edge such as `ready->queued`.

   The system-required set is enforced in two places: the CRUD validator rejects disabling/deleting these edges (`FailedPrecondition`), and the selector unions them into the configured graph at read time, and into the Default graph on the fallback path (nil `Configured`, empty cache, load error; Repair round 5), so a legacy database that already disabled one cannot strand items and a failed cache load cannot deny archive. The set does not exist in the tree yet (`git grep` returns zero hits); Task 1.1.2b creates it. A code-defined set avoids an ent schema change and any migration, and the equivalent of a `system_required` marker in the DB would drift from the lifecycle code that is the real source of truth.
4. **The one swallowing caller** (`claimAndSpawnCandidate`) gets a shared helper `transitionOrNotify` in package `server/services` (new file `backlog_transition_notify.go`), scoped to service-layer callers. It is NOT placed in `session`: no `session` code is an adopter after the site re-derivation, and `session` cannot import `server/services`, so the cycle in the earlier two-adopter design is avoided by scope, not by an interface. On a denial (`ErrTransitionNotAllowed`, a new typed sentinel wrapped at `transitionWithGuard`, or `ErrGateNotSatisfied`) it records the distinct stuck reason `transition_denied`, posts a visible comment and `notify()` once per `(item, caller, from, to, reason)`, and never swallows silently (memory: document AI decisions in edge cases). If a `session`-package adopter appears later, move the helper behind a small `TransitionDeniedReporter` interface injected from the service layer. The claim-error `switch` is first extracted into `handleClaimError` to keep the gocognit/gocyclo new-code gate green.
5. **Engine holders**: BacklogService and SyncLoop both receive the selector (new `SyncLoop.SetWorkflowEngine`); the lifecycle listener keeps `stageConfigEngine` because it uses it only to resolve automated-review/custom-check gate context, not to decide legality. A wiring test pins all three; the stale comment at `session/backlog_lifecycle.go:271-274` is corrected.

## Alternatives rejected

- [Reversed by ADR-007; kept for history] Route every direct-storage site through the engine now: touches `tools_backlog.go` (large), `autonomous_orchestration_service.go`, lifecycle, Jules poller and the `silenttransition` linter's expectations; high regression risk on the autonomous pipeline for a flag-gated, unproven change. Revisit only if the bypass counter shows operators editing edges the automation relies on. (Revisited and reversed by the owner on 2026-10-17.)
- A `system_required` boolean on `StageTransition`: needs ent schema and migration for something the code already knows.

## Consequences

- [Superseded] Success Metric 1 was scoped to service-level transitions; under ADR-007 it covers all status-write paths, per caller class.
- Autonomous completion still skips the AC-required gate on triage-to-ready (`autonomous_orchestration_service.go:586`); that gap is pre-existing, now counted, and not fixed here.
