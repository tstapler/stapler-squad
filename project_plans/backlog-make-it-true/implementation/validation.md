## Happy Path Scenario

Given a fresh stapler-squad instance with seeded stage config, shadow mode on and every enforce flag off (Baseline: stage edits change nothing and the stuck panel says "unknown"), when the operator disables the non-system-required edge `done->refining` in Backlog Stages and flips `backlog:workflow-engine-enforce` on (confirm dialog), then `TransitionBacklogItemStatus(done->refining)` is denied and, with the edge re-enabled, allowed again without restart, while the Stages banner reads "Partly enforced" until the agent and automation flags are also on; when the operator then adds a `human_approval` gate on `pr_pending->done` and flips `backlog:workflow-engine-enforce-system` on, a PR-merged item stays `pr_pending` with a `transition_denied` stuck reason until the gate is approved, then moves to `done` on the next tick; the quota page saves a budget that the next `QuotaGate.Reconcile` uses, and a stuck `pr_ready_unmerged` item renders a real "Repo auto-merge: ..." line.
