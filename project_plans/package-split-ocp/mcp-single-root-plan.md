# server/mcp single composition root (owner decision Q5, 2026-11-20)

Owner decision: `server/mcp` is NOT a second composition root. It receives everything from `server/dependencies.go` and may not name concrete `session`, `server/services` or `github` types or build its own deps. This file measures the gap (the Measurement section is the origin/main 196dc0e74 baseline, not live counts) and tracks the follow-up rows; each row's status is in its own cell.

Note: `tierc-roadmap.md` has its own Q4/Q5 (SC6 waiver, go-list counts); they are unrelated to this Q4/Q5.

## Reading of the rule

- Already compliant: no long-lived dep (store, service, bus, cache, storage) is constructed in `server/mcp`.
- Gaps against Q5: (1) the stdio fallback builds deps in `main.go`, outside `dependencies.go` (closed by M5); (2) two self-built `wakes.NewMetrics()` and two `git.NewGitWorktreeFromStorage`; (3) direct `githubpkg.Get*` calls; (4) concrete types in the `NewCore` signature and in handler fields; (5) `tool_modules.go:37` called itself "the one place server/mcp acts as a second composition root" (rewritten by M6). (1)-(3) are closed by M1-M3 and M5; (4) is open as M4b.
- Open reading for the owner to confirm in review: whether `defaultToolModules` (module assembly over injected deps) counts as "building its own deps". This plan assumes it does not, and that `server/dependencies.go` only has to hand in the deps and the extra modules via `WithToolModules`.
