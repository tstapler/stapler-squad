# Notifications

Stapler Squad tells you when a session needs attention: an approval request, a question, an error, or a finished task. Notifications appear as toasts and are kept in the notification tray, which you open from the bell in the header.

Open the full list on the [Notifications](/notifications) page. Pending decisions are pinned and do not time out. **Move all to tray** clears the screen without marking anything read, and an undo bar stays briefly after you use it.

Toast timing is stored per browser, not on your account. For the full behaviour of the toast deck and the tray, see the notifications how-to in the repository at `docs/how-to/manage-notifications.md`.
